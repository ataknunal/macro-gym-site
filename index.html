What it does
  • Builds a weekly program for your goal, experience and equipment
  • Logs sets, weights and rest, and suggests your next weight
  • Tracks calories and macros: add food by search, barcode or photo
  • Shows your progress: weight trend, records, measurements
  • Works without an account; your data stays on your phone

Support

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: write to volumsport@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Core workout and nutrition tracking works in guest mode. Food search and barcode lookups use external product data services. An account adds cloud backup and is required for photo scanning and Plus purchases.

Where does my meal photo go?

Only if you use “Scan from photo” and allow it: the photo is sent to Google Gemini to recognize the food. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Without the app, see account deletion.

Recommendations in Macro Gym are for general information only and are not medical advice.
