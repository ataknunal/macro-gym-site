Membership Terms

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · Developer: Atakan Ünal

Age and access

Macro Gym is only for adults aged 18 and over. You must provide your date of birth and an accurate age declaration. Declaration checks are not identity verification. Age restrictions do not prevent account deletion or management of store subscriptions.

Free features, Plus and Pro

Workout program creation and editing, workout logs, calorie and macro tracking, food search, manual entry and barcodes are free. Photo scanning requires sign-in. The free welcome period starts with the first scan: 3 scans per day for 7 days, then 1 per day. Plus includes 3 food scans per day and 1 program scan in total per account. Renewing, resubscribing or returning to Plus does not grant another Plus program scan. Pro includes 5 food scans per day and 2 program scans per month, including on annual subscriptions. Program scans have a separate allowance and count when a usable reading is returned. Unused daily and monthly scans do not roll over. An unused Plus program scan is preserved for the same account and can be used during an active Plus membership.

A single photo counts as one scan even if it contains several foods. Scans without a usable result do not consume your daily allowance; additional limits on unsuccessful attempts and abusive network traffic apply. The plans screen shows your remaining allowance and reset time. Photo results are estimates, not medical advice.

Payment and renewal

Payments are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. The store displays the price, currency and billing period before confirmation. Monthly plans are charged each month; annual plans are charged in full once per year. The monthly equivalent of an annual price is not a payment instalment. Subscriptions automatically renew until cancelled. No payment card is required for the free scan allowance.

Cancellation and refunds

You can manage or cancel renewal in the store account used to purchase, or select “Manage subscription” on the plans screen. Cancel before the next renewal to avoid the next charge; access continues for the paid period, subject to store refunds or revocation. Refunds follow the store's rules and applicable consumer rights.

Deleting the app or your Macro Gym account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it separately. Account deletion requests deletion of the RevenueCat customer; the provider processes this asynchronously. Legally required store transaction records may be retained, as explained in the privacy policy.

Restoring purchases

Sign in with the Macro Gym account used for purchase and use “Restore purchases”. Use the same App Store or Google Play account. If verification is delayed, refresh membership details before attempting another payment. Contact support if you cannot recover your account. A subscription is associated with one Macro Gym account.

iOS application licence

The iOS application uses Apple's Standard End User Licence Agreement. These subscription details explain Plus and Pro access and billing alongside that licence.
