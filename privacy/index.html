Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026
Summary

Macro Gym (formerly Volüm) is local-first: the primary copy of your data lives on your phone. Core tracking works without an account. Food search and barcode queries connect to product data services; photo scanning and subscription purchases require sign-in. Your data is never sold and never used for advertising; the app contains no ad networks or advertising tracking SDKs. Sentry error reporting and session tracking are disabled in the current release.

1. Who collects data

The data controller for the Macro Gym app is Atakan Ünal (individual developer).
Contact: volumsport@gmail.com

2. Our approach

The primary copy of your data is on your own phone. Workout and nutrition tracking work in guest mode. Search and barcode lookups use external product data services. Photo scanning and subscription purchases require an account.

If you create an account, your data is also written to the cloud (Google Firebase) for account management and backup. Your account ID is also used to verify photo allowances and membership access.

3. Data we collect

3.1 Account information

  • Your email address and password for sign-in. Google Firebase Authentication manages password credentials; Macro Gym does not keep a separate password copy.
  • Your display name.

3.2 Health and fitness data

  • Height, weight, birth year, sex, goal, activity and experience level
  • Body measurements (waist, chest, arms, etc.)
  • Workout logs: exercises, sets, weights, reps, durations
  • Nutrition logs: foods eaten, calories, macronutrients and, in Türkiye, food spending
  • Water intake and workout streak
  • Supplement plans, selected days, target amounts and intake entries

Supplement plans and intake entries use the same local and, when signed in, private Firebase storage as other fitness records. Evening reminders are optional local phone notifications. Reminder preferences stay on this device; no push token or supplement details are sent to a notification service. Lock screen messages do not name supplements. Plans and intake entries are included in account data deletion.

3.3 Photos

Workout images: A workout plan selected from your gallery is sent to Google Gemini through our Cloudflare relay only after you give separate permission for that upload. The relay does not store the image. Extracted days, exercises and notes become workout data only after you review and save the plan. The paid API data terms explained below for meal photos also apply.

  • Progress photos: never leave your phone. The app only stores the photo's file path on your device and never uploads the image.
  • Meal photos: if you use “Scan from photo”, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini service to recognize the food in it (see section 4). If you never use this feature, nothing is sent. The photo is resized on your phone, then sent to our relay server running on Cloudflare, which forwards it to Google; the relay does not store the photo. We use the paid Gemini API. Under Google's paid-service terms, prompts, photos and responses are not used to improve its products or train its models. Google may temporarily retain them for safety, abuse prevention and legal requirements. Only include the meal in the photo.
    Before the first scan, the app tells you the photo will go to Google Gemini and asks for your explicit permission; without it, no photo is sent. You can withdraw permission at any time with the Profile → Photo scan permission switch.
    This feature requires an account: the relay verifies your session so it can limit how much each person uses it. It is not available in guest mode.

3.4 Crash reports

Sentry error reporting is disabled in the current release. No app error reports, screen recordings or session tracking are sent to Sentry. Server-side technical logs and network-address counters used for security and abuse prevention are separate from this disabled app telemetry.

4.1 Visiting this website

The public support and policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security, including visits made without a GitHub account. This website has no sign-in, payment form or analytics scripts. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement. These website visits are separate from the data processed by the mobile app.

5. Deleting your data

From the app

Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes all of:

  • Your Firebase account (email and password)
  • All of your workout, nutrition, measurement and water logs in the cloud
  • The local copy on this device

This cannot be undone and deleted data cannot be recovered.

To delete only the copy on this device and keep your account, use Profile → Clear data on this device.

If you deleted the app

If you no longer have access to the app, email us from your account's address; your account and all your data will be deleted within 30 days. Steps:

Account Deletion Request

Deleting your account or the app does not cancel an App Store/Google Play subscription; manage it separately through your store account. The app requests deletion of your RevenueCat customer before deleting the Firebase account. RevenueCat processes deletion asynchronously. Daily security counters and a temporary deletion marker are removed within 48 hours. Stores and payment providers may retain transaction records where required by law. Contact support for requests outside the app.

6. Data retention

Data is kept until you delete it. Removing the app from your phone does not delete your cloud data — follow the steps above to delete it.

7. Children's privacy

Macro Gym is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. Before using the app, users provide a date of birth and an explicit 18+ age declaration. The full date is processed temporarily to calculate age, is not stored, and is not sent to Gemini. The device stores the birth year and declaration; the server stores the declaration version, adult acceptance and acceptance time linked to the account ID. The server rejects photo analysis without a declaration. These checks rely on user-declared information and are not identity or verified-age checks. Account deletion and support remain accessible.

8. Health disclaimer

Calorie, macro and workout recommendations in the app are for general information only and are not medical advice. If you have a health condition, talk to your doctor before changing your diet or training.

9. Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkish KVKK you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data. All of your data is already visible and editable in the app; for deletion see section 5. For other requests, write to volumsport@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above is updated and significant changes are announced in the app.
