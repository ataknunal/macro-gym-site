Account and Data Deletion

App: Macro Gym (formerly Volüm) · Developer: Atakan Ünal
Two ways

1. If the app is still on your phone: Profile → Delete account. Deletion is immediate, no waiting.
2. If you deleted the app: write to the address below and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open Macro Gym and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the Profile tab at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm it's you.
  5. Tap Permanently delete my account.

If you only want to delete the copy on this phone and keep your account, use Profile → Clear data on this device instead.

Option 2 — Email request

If you can't access the app (you changed phones, deleted the app or can't sign in), write to us from the email address your account is registered with:

Email volumsport@gmail.com

To verify the request, it must be sent from the same email address as your account. Your account and all your data will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase account (email and password)
  • Your profile: height, weight, birth year, sex, goal, experience and activity level
  • All of your workout logs, your program and your streak
  • All of your nutrition, water and body measurement logs
  • The local copy on that phone

Deletion cannot be undone; deleted data cannot be recovered or restored from a backup.

What is kept

The app requests deletion of your RevenueCat customer before deleting your Firebase account. Provider deletion is processed asynchronously. Security counters and the temporary deletion marker are removed within 48 hours. Stores and payment providers may retain transaction records where legally required.

Deleting your account or the app does not cancel your subscription. Manage and cancel it separately in the App Store or Google Play account used for purchase.

Progress photos are never uploaded to the cloud in the first place; they stay only on your phone and go away when you delete the app.
